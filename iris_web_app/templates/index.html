📚 How to Use This App

🔮 Making Predictions

  1. Enter the flower measurements in centimeters:
    • Sepal Length: Length of the sepal (typical range: 4.3 - 7.9 cm)
    • Sepal Width: Width of the sepal (typical range: 2.0 - 4.4 cm)
    • Petal Length: Length of the petal (typical range: 1.0 - 6.9 cm)
    • Petal Width: Width of the petal (typical range: 0.1 - 2.5 cm)
  2. Click the "Predict Species" button
  3. View the prediction result with confidence percentage

📊 Viewing Charts

Click the chart buttons to visualize the Iris dataset:

  • 2D Charts: View scatter plots and feature distributions
  • 3D Chart: Interactive 3D visualization of the dataset
  • 3D PCA: Principal Component Analysis in 3D

🌙 Dark Mode

Click the sun/moon button in the navbar to toggle between light and dark themes. Your preference is saved automatically!
